About this system

AI-Based Integrated Safety And Risk System For Smart Workspace. It watches the workplace through a camera and tracks the employee buses, then shows both in one place.

PPE and danger zone

A trained YOLOv8 model finds each person and checks for a helmet, vest, boots and gloves. A zone drawn on the camera view marks the area where PPE is mandatory.

Stress estimate

A Teachable Machine model reads the facial expression of each person and labels it stressed or normal. Several frames are averaged so the label does not flicker.

Employee transport

Each bus sends its GPS position. The system compares it with the planned route and raises an alert for a route deviation, an unexpected stop, a lost signal or a missed stop.

How a person's status is decided

PPEPosition and stressStatus
CompleteStress not highSafe
CompleteStress highCaution
MissingOutside the danger zone, stress not highPPE violation
MissingInside the danger zoneHigh risk
MissingOutside the zone, stress highHigh risk

An alert is saved only when a problem lasts for a few frames, and the same alert is not repeated for 10 seconds.

Built with

Python, OpenCV, YOLOv8 (Ultralytics), Teachable Machine with TensorFlow Lite, Flask, SQLite and Leaflet for the map.

What to keep in mind

  • The stress label comes from facial expression only. It is a risk indicator, not a medical measurement of stress.
  • The stress model was trained on a public expression dataset, so accuracy on a webcam is lower than on its own test pictures.
  • Buses in the demo are simulated unless a phone or GPS device sends real positions.
